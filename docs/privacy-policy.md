# Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 16, 2026

Community Achievements is a Devvit Web application for Reddit communities. This Privacy Policy explains what data the app processes when it is installed in a subreddit.

## Data We Process

Community Achievements may store:

- Subreddit configuration, including enabled features, thresholds, recap settings, excluded users, and display preferences.
- Achievement definitions created by moderators.
- Aggregate activity counters such as post counts, comment counts, weekly activity counts, report counters, and achievement progress.
- Badge records, including username, badge name, reason, award source, visibility, subreddit name, flair status, and award date.
- Achievement unlock records for community, user, and post milestones.
- Event logs and moderator audit logs for app actions, errors, awards, recap generation, settings changes, and payment fulfillment.
- Weekly recap snapshots, including draft or published recap content.
- Optional positive leaderboard entries based on badge counts.
- Supporter or premium entitlement status when a user supports the app through Reddit/Devvit payments.
- Payment order identifiers needed to fulfill purchases idempotently and handle refunds.

## Data We Do Not Collect

Community Achievements does not intentionally collect or infer sensitive personal categories such as race, religion, health, political beliefs, sexual orientation, or precise location.

The app does not collect payment card details. Purchases are handled by Reddit/Devvit payment systems, and the app only receives order and product information needed to deliver cosmetic or premium features.

The app does not use data to manipulate Reddit votes, karma, ranking, or recommendation systems.

## How Data Is Used

Data is used to:

- Show moderator dashboards.
- Track community achievement progress.
- Award and display badges.
- Generate weekly recaps.
- Maintain public achievement galleries if enabled by moderators.
- Provide optional positive leaderboards if enabled.
- Fulfill cosmetic supporter badges and premium moderator features.
- Keep audit logs for moderation accountability and troubleshooting.

## Public Visibility

Some data may be public inside the subreddit when moderators enable public features, including achievement galleries, public badge cards, recap posts, and leaderboards.

Private badge visibility, when enabled, is intended to show a badge only to the badge owner and moderators.

## Data Sharing

Community Achievements does not sell personal data. Data is processed within Reddit/Devvit infrastructure and may be visible to Reddit as part of normal platform operation.

Subreddit moderators may view moderator dashboard data, badge records, audit logs, and aggregate statistics for their installed community.

## Data Retention

Community Achievements keeps operational records for as long as needed to provide the app experience, maintain auditability, and support subreddit history. Some logs and snapshots are capped to limit storage growth.

Moderators can disable tracking and automatic publishing from the app settings. Historical records may remain available unless removed through future administrative tooling or by uninstalling/resetting app data where supported.

## User Choices

Users may:

- View their badges from the app gallery.
- Use badge visibility controls if enabled by the subreddit.
- Avoid supporter purchases; paid features are optional and cosmetic or moderator-focused.

Moderators may:

- Disable the app.
- Disable auto tracking.
- Disable auto publishing.
- Disable public gallery or leaderboard features.
- Exclude specific users from awards.
- Revoke badges.

## Children

Community Achievements is intended for use within Reddit communities and is subject to Reddit’s platform rules and age requirements.

## Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the app evolves. Material changes should be reflected in this repository and, when appropriate, communicated to moderators.

## Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, open an issue in the GitHub repository for Community Achievements or contact the app developer through the published Reddit developer listing.
